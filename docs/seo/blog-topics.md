# 100 Blog Topics

Organised into six clusters. Each cluster has a **pillar** (a long, comprehensive
page that the supporting posts link up to) and supporting posts that each target
one long-tail query.

Every topic below is chosen because someone actually types something like it into
Google *and* you can answer it from real experience. Topics you would have to
research from scratch are marked ⚠ — write those last, or not at all. Thin posts
on subjects you don't know are worse than no posts.

**Format:** `#. Title — target query` · *(intent)*

## How to sequence these

**First ten**, in this order — each one supports a service page that carries real
commercial value, and each is answerable purely from experience:

1 · 24 · 41 · 21 · 61 · 83 · 3 · 26 · 81 · 96

**Cadence.** One genuinely good post every two weeks beats four rushed ones a
month. Topical authority comes from depth and internal linking, not volume.

**Length.** Aim 1,200–2,000 words for supporting posts. Do not pad — a tight
900-word post that fully answers the query outranks a padded 2,500-word one.

**The ⚠ topics** (73, 89, 94) need research or opinions you may not want to publish
under your own name while employed. Skip them unless you're confident.
